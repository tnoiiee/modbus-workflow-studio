## Resource bounds actually implemented

- Snapshot: 1–200 raw IDs, 32 KiB body, **512 KiB response**, no-store; 20/sec burst 20;
  8 concurrent bodies with 5s deadline.
- Journal: **16,384 events / 16 MiB serialized / 60s**; no subscriber pinning.
- Connection: **256 queued frames / 1 MiB queued / 1 MiB buffered / 64 KiB frame**;
  incoming controls also 64 KiB (200-ID sources plus canonical selectionKey must fit).
- **4 subscriptions / 500 distinct IDs per connection**, **16 connections**, **2,000 global
  identities including snapshot/resume leases**, **200 raw IDs per subscribe**.
- 10s without applied ACK progress **while delivery pending**, never an unchanged-idle timeout;
  256 outstanding boundaries/subscription. Shared 50ms pump, up to 128 scanned events each range;
  filtered checkpoints at most every 250ms. Per-socket churn/invalid limits are in the scope document.
- Headless: **1 subscription / 200 cached items / 512 KiB cache**, 64 pending frames/512 KiB,
  64 KiB incoming frame; one socket/request/apply callback, at most three timers, one 128-char error.
  Backoff 250ms→30s ceiling ±20% jitter (up to 36s actual), attempt counter ≤8.
- Origin configurable; missing-Origin clients allowed by default, optionally disabled. Not auth.

Payload-byte bounds are not total heap/RSS bounds. Memory-only replay, leases and cap rejection are
intentional. A reconnect after expiry or after watch recreation needs a snapshot; larger selections
or data than an applicable cap need reduction, not truncation. No exactly-once/durable/HA guarantee.
